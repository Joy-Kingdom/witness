# The creative system - "Everything testifies"

One idea runs through every pixel: **the check**. WITNESS is a product about
a contract checking a payment against an owner's word - so in this product,
*nothing is decorative*. Every motion is a verification act:

- **Loading is the Nine Checks.** There is no spinner anywhere. When the
  chain is being read, the contract's nine checks tick in order - Not
  revoked · Not consumed · Not expired · Version match · Recipient match ·
  Within cap · Invoice fingerprint · Mandate binding · Vault balance.
  Waiting feels like the product working, because it is.
- **Verdicts are stamped, not faded.** DENIED and PAID land like a rubber
  stamp on a case file - a physical act of record, with an ink ring. A
  refusal is paperwork that will outlive the demo.
- **The changed word flips.** Switching between the denied and the paid
  case highlights the single swapped word - `agent` / `supplier` - under a
  lamp sweep, and the ≠ sign jumps. One word is the whole crime; the eye
  is taught to look for exactly that word.
- **The Thread.** A thin teal line across the top fills as you scroll -
  one evidence thread tying Case W/001 to Evidence W/002, the way the
  mandate ties the invoice to the payment.
- **Buttons press like stamps.** Every press is a small act of record:
  it goes down, it leaves a mark, it cannot be taken back quietly.
- **Skeleton evidence.** While the observer reads the chain, the record
  shimmers like paper under a scanner - never a blank void pretending
  nothing exists.

Vocabulary is one register everywhere: APPROVED / PROPOSED / CHECKING /
DENIED / PAID. Case-file numbering (W/001, W/002), the teal-and-lime
shield, the serif of a document that expects to be audited.

Reduced-motion is respected: with `prefers-reduced-motion`, the testimony
is instant and still complete.

Files: `app/witness-motion.css`, `app/witness-motion.js` - purely
presentational; they never touch data, verdicts or requests.
